Every day is part fun, part function.

Children learn best when they're having a good time. So we fill the day with play, and we build everyday skills right into it.

The Fun

what the kids notice

  • Art and crayonsPainting, drawing, play dough and plenty of glue.
  • Outside every dayBackyard play, nature walks and puddle jumping, weather permitting.
  • Music and movementSongs, dancing, shakers and drums.
  • Pretend and buildPlay kitchen, dress-up, blocks and trains.
  • StoriesPicture books every day and a cozy reading corner.
+

The Function

what they're learning

  • Taking care of myselfWashing hands, putting on shoes and coats, using the potty.
  • Strong little handsHolding a crayon, cutting with scissors, buttons and zippers.
  • Getting alongSharing, taking turns, and using words for big feelings.
  • Ready for schoolLetters, numbers, shapes, colors and following a routine.
  • Helping outSetting the table, watering plants, cleaning up after play.

Fun + Function = a happy kid who is ready for kindergarten.

A day at Fun and Function

Kids do best with a rhythm they can count on. Every part of the day is a little fun, a little function, and often both.

A typical weekday

7:00 am to 5:30 pm

Monday through Friday. Times are a guide, not a stopwatch.

  • Fun
  • Function
  • Fun + Function

Infants keep their own feeding and nap schedule from home.

  1. 7:00am

    Arrival and free play Fun

    Hellos at the door, then puzzles, blocks and books while friends arrive.

  2. 8:00am

    Breakfast Function

    Wash hands, find our seats, and pour and serve with a little help.

  3. 8:45am

    Circle time Fun + Function

    Good-morning song, the calendar, the weather and today's plan.

  4. 9:15am

    Learning centers Fun

    Art, blocks, the sensory table, the play kitchen and small-group lessons.

  5. 10:15am

    Clean-up and snack Function

    Everyone helps put the toys back where they live.

  6. 10:30am

    Outdoor play Fun + Function

    Running, climbing and the sandbox. Coats and shoes on by ourselves.

  7. 11:45am

    Lunch Function

    Family-style meal. Trying new foods and practicing table manners.

  8. 12:30pm

    Nap and quiet time Function

    Each child sets up their own cot and blanket. Quiet books for kids who don't nap.

  9. 2:45pm

    Snack and story Fun + Function

    A healthy snack, then a read-aloud on the rug.

  10. 3:15pm

    Projects and music Fun

    Art projects and dancing. School-age friends arrive for snack and homework time.

  11. 4:15pm

    Outside or free play Fun

    Back outside when the weather is nice, or games and building inside.

  12. 5:00pm

    Pickup Function

    Gather our things and say goodbye. Photos and notes from the day are in the parent app.

Care for every age

Babies through school-age kids, together in one home. Older kids help, little ones watch and learn, and brothers and sisters can stay together.

Infants

6 weeks – 18 months

We follow your baby's feeding and nap schedule from home. Lots of floor time, songs and cuddles, with a daily note on bottles, diapers and naps.

Toddlers

18 months – 3 years

Busy bodies and first words. Simple routines, potty learning when they're ready, climbing, scribbling and stories.

Preschool

3 – 5 years

Letters, numbers and projects, plus the skills kindergarten teachers look for: listening, taking turns and zipping a coat.

School-age

5 – 12 years

Before and after school, school breaks and summer. Snack, homework time and plenty of room to play.

What parents ask about

  • Licensed and inspected

    Licensed by the New York State Office of Children and Family Services (OCFS) and inspected on a regular schedule.

  • Trained for safety

    CPR and First Aid certified. Monthly fire drills, plus smoke and carbon monoxide detectors throughout the home.

  • Meals included

    Breakfast, lunch and snacks made here, following USDA child nutrition guidelines.

  • Updates all day

    Photos, meals, naps and notes sent to your phone through our parent app.

  • Help paying for care

    We accept child care assistance through Orange County DSS. Ask us how to apply.

  • A real home

    A small group, a calm routine, and the same caring face every day.
