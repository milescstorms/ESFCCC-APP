one home, every age

A cubby for everybody

Babies through school-age kids, together in one home. Older kids help, little ones learn by watching, and brothers and sisters get to stay together.

Infants

6 weeks – 18 months

We follow your baby's feeding and nap schedule from home. Lots of floor time, songs and cuddles, with a daily note on bottles, diapers and naps.

Toddlers

18 months – 3 years

Busy bodies and first words. Simple routines, potty learning when they're ready, climbing, scribbling and stories.

Preschool

3 – 5 years

Letters, numbers and projects, plus the skills kindergarten teachers look for: listening, taking turns and zipping a coat.

School-age

5 – 12 years

Before and after school, school breaks and summer. Snack, homework time and plenty of room to play.

What parents ask us

  • Are you licensed?

    Yes. We're licensed by the New York State Office of Children and Family Services (OCFS) and inspected on a regular schedule.

  • How do you keep kids safe?

    CPR and First Aid certified. Monthly fire drills, plus smoke and carbon monoxide detectors throughout the home.

  • What do the kids eat?

    Breakfast, lunch and snacks, made here and following the USDA MyPlate guidelines.

  • Will I hear how the day went?

    Yes. Photos, meals, naps and notes come to your phone through our parent app.

  • Can I get help paying?

    We accept child care assistance through Orange County DSS. Ask us and we'll walk you through applying.

  • What does it cost?

    Tuition depends on your child's age and schedule. Full-time and part-time spots are available. We'll go over rates at your tour.
