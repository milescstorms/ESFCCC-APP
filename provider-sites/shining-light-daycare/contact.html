Questions parents ask

Do you have openings right now?

Spots are limited because we care for a small group. Send a tour request with your child's age and start date and we'll tell you what's open. If we're full, we can add you to the waitlist.

Can I stop by without an appointment?

Visits are by appointment only. This is our home, and the children's safety comes first. You'll get the address when your tour is confirmed.

Can I use child care assistance?

Let us know on the form if you plan to use child care assistance through the Orange County Department of Social Services. If you need help applying, the Child Care Council of Orange County can walk you through it.

What should I bring to the tour?

Just your questions. Your child is welcome to come along so you can see how they feel in the space.
