What the guide covers

Four parts of the New York State child care regulations (18 NYCRR).

  • Part 415Child Care Assistance & Legally Exempt
  • Part 416Group Family Day Care
  • Part 417Family Day Care
  • Part 418-1Day Care Centers

Tips for better answers

  1. Say what kind of program you have: family, group family, center, or legally exempt.
  2. Ask one question at a time, in your own words.
  3. Look up the section number it gives you, and confirm with your registrar or licensor.
