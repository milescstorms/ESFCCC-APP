Empire State Family Child Care CollaborativeMember App Guide, 1 of 3

Admins

Miles (ESFCCC Business Coach). Admins can do everything on the Staff sheet, plus the items below.

Settings tab

  • Only admins can see and change Settings.
  • Turn automatic renewal emails on or off.
  • Run Check who is due today to see which memberships (MOUs) are coming up for renewal before emails go out.

Approvals

  • Approve or decline attendee changes members ask for after a training has happened.
  • Approved changes update that facility's training hours.

Logins

  • Member and staff logins are private. Never post them or share one member's login with another.
  • Hand out staff logins, one per person.
  • Members can change their own password or reset it from the sign-in screen.

Records and backups

  • Every uploaded certificate is emailed, with the file attached, to the app's Gmail. Subjects start with Certificate backup.
  • Deleting a certificate in the app does not delete the email copy.
  • Export the member list to Excel once a month to keep a copy.
Good to know

The app's Gmail sends about 100 emails a day. Sign-up emails, renewal reminders and certificate backups all share that limit.

Empire State Family Child Care CollaborativeMember App Guide, 2 of 3

Staff

Every staff member has their own login. The app works on a phone, with a full dashboard on a computer.

Members

  • Search every member and non-member facility.
  • Open a record to see contact info, membership status, trainings, hours and certificates.
  • Add a new member and pick their software (Brightwheel or Playground). They can sign in right away.
  • Turn a facility on the Not members yet list into a member.
  • View or delete certificates on a record. Deleting is permanent.

Newsletters and questions

  • Publish newsletters for members to read in the app.

Resources

  • Add a guide, form or link: a title, one line on what it is, and the link (Google Drive, PDF or website).
  • Sort it into Software, Benefits, Business or Forms.
  • It saves as a draft only staff can see. Tap Publish when it is ready.
  • Delete anything that is out of date. The Brightwheel and Playground links are built in.
Privacy

Member contact info is for ESFCCC staff only. Never share one member's details with another.

Empire State Family Child Care CollaborativeMember App Guide, 3 of 3

Members

Sign in with your ESFCCC member ID and password. Tap the refresh button at the top of any page to load the latest info.

Resources

  • Open guides, forms and links from your coach under Resources.
  • They are sorted into Software, Benefits, Business and Forms.
  • Tap the refresh button to see anything your coach just added.

Your account

  • Change your password anytime, or reset it if you forget.

Membership

  • Your member card shows your software (Brightwheel or Playground), your status and your membership year. Tap it to open your software.
  • See every benefit your membership includes. Your coach helps you renew your MOU each year.

Hours and certificates

  • See training hours for each person who attended.
  • Upload certificates as a PDF or photo, with the type and expiration date.

Your coach and news

  • Call or email your coach from the Coach tab.
  • Read ESFCCC newsletters and updates.
Need help signing in?

Call your coach at (845) 294-4012 ext. 230 and we will get you signed in or reset your password.

Your privacy

Only ESFCCC staff can see your contact info. It is never shared with other members.
